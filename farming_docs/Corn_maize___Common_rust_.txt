CROP: Corn (Maize)
DISEASE: Common Rust
PATHOGEN/CAUSE: Puccinia sorghi (fungus)

SYMPTOMS:
Small, reddish-brown, elongated pustules scattered on both leaf surfaces, which rupture and release rust-colored spores. Severe infections can cause leaf yellowing and premature death, reducing yield.

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS FOR SPREAD:
Cool temperatures (16-23°C) with high humidity or dew. Spores are wind-dispersed over long distances.

ORGANIC / NON-CHEMICAL TREATMENT:
Choose resistant hybrids as the primary organic strategy, since rust is difficult to manage post-infection without fungicides. Monitor fields regularly during cool, humid periods.

CHEMICAL TREATMENT:
Fungicides containing strobilurins or triazoles applied at early pustule appearance, particularly on susceptible hybrids or in high-risk conditions. Confirm timing and product with local extension guidance.
(Note: Always confirm exact product names and dosages with a local agricultural extension officer or product label, as recommendations vary by region and formulation.)

PREVENTION:
Plant rust-resistant corn hybrids, which are widely available and highly effective. Monitor weather conditions favorable for rust (cool, humid) and scout fields regularly during those periods.
