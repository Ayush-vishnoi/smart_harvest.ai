CROP: Tomato
DISEASE: Early Blight
PATHOGEN/CAUSE: Alternaria solani (fungus)

SYMPTOMS:
Dark brown spots with concentric rings (target-like pattern) on older, lower leaves first. Yellowing around spots, leaf drop progressing upward. Can also affect stems (dark lesions) and fruit (sunken dark spots near the stem end).

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS FOR SPREAD:
Warm temperatures (24-29°C), high humidity, alternating wet and dry periods. Spreads through wind, rain splash, and infected debris.

ORGANIC / NON-CHEMICAL TREATMENT:
Neem oil or copper-based sprays applied at early symptom onset. Remove infected lower leaves promptly. Mulch to reduce soil splash onto leaves.

CHEMICAL TREATMENT:
Fungicides containing chlorothalonil or mancozeb, applied on a preventive schedule once conditions favor disease. Confirm exact dosage with local extension guidelines.
(Note: Always confirm exact product names and dosages with a local agricultural extension officer or product label, as recommendations vary by region and formulation.)

PREVENTION:
Rotate crops (avoid planting after other solanaceous crops for 2+ years). Stake or cage plants for airflow. Water at the base, not overhead. Ensure balanced fertilization; avoid nitrogen excess/potassium deficiency, which increases susceptibility.
