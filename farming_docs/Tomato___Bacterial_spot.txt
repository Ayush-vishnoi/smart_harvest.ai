CROP: Tomato
DISEASE: Bacterial Spot
PATHOGEN/CAUSE: Xanthomonas campestris pv. vesicatoria (bacterium)

SYMPTOMS:
Small, water-soaked spots on leaves that turn dark brown to black with a yellow halo. Spots may also appear on stems and fruit as raised, scab-like lesions. Severely affected leaves yellow and drop, reducing yield.

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS FOR SPREAD:
Warm, humid weather (24-30°C) with prolonged leaf wetness from rain or overhead irrigation. Spreads via splashing water, contaminated tools, and infected seed.

ORGANIC / NON-CHEMICAL TREATMENT:
Copper-based bactericide sprays applied preventively at first sign of disease. Remove and destroy infected plant debris. Avoid overhead watering; water at the base instead.

CHEMICAL TREATMENT:
Copper hydroxide or copper oxychloride based fungicide-bactericide sprays, applied on a regular schedule during humid periods. Follow label instructions and consult a local agricultural officer for exact dosage suited to your region and formulation.
(Note: Always confirm exact product names and dosages with a local agricultural extension officer or product label, as recommendations vary by region and formulation.)

PREVENTION:
Use certified disease-free seed and transplants. Rotate crops with non-solanaceous plants for 2-3 years. Avoid working in fields when foliage is wet. Space plants for good air circulation. Disinfect tools between plants.
