CROP: Potato
DISEASE: Early Blight
PATHOGEN/CAUSE: Alternaria solani (fungus)

SYMPTOMS:
Dark brown spots with concentric rings on older leaves first, surrounded by a yellow halo. Progresses upward, causing significant leaf loss. Can also cause dark, sunken lesions on tubers.

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS FOR SPREAD:
Warm temperatures with alternating wet and dry periods; stressed or nutrient-deficient plants are more susceptible.

ORGANIC / NON-CHEMICAL TREATMENT:
Copper-based fungicide sprays at first symptoms. Remove infected foliage. Maintain balanced soil fertility to reduce plant stress and susceptibility.

CHEMICAL TREATMENT:
Fungicides containing chlorothalonil or mancozeb applied on a preventive schedule during the growing season. Confirm application rates with local agricultural guidance.
(Note: Always confirm exact product names and dosages with a local agricultural extension officer or product label, as recommendations vary by region and formulation.)

PREVENTION:
Rotate crops with non-solanaceous plants for 2-3 years. Ensure adequate plant nutrition, especially nitrogen and potassium. Avoid overhead irrigation. Remove volunteer potato plants and crop debris after harvest.
